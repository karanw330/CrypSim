THE DETAILS

A few things
worth knowing.

Am I trading real cryptocurrency?+

No. CrypSim simulates orders with virtual money. You won't be making real cryptocurrency purchases.

How much virtual money do I get?+

New CrypSim accounts start with $100,000 in virtual funds.

Which assets can I trade?+

The trading interface supports BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP and DOGE.
